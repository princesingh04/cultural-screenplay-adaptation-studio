### [COST_AMAR_01] Field Work Kurta & Tweed Waistcoat
- **Character**: Amarjit Singh (`CHAR_AMAR`)
- **Variant Number**: 1
- **Garments**: Unbleached coarse Khadi cotton kurta, olive twill trousers/chaadra, brown tweed waistcoat with deep inner document pocket.
- **Regional Fabrics**: Handspun coarse Khadi, winter wool tweed.
- **Color Palette**: `#e2e8f0` (Raw Khadi), `#78350f` (Brown Tweed), `#334155` (Slate).
- **Footwear**: Handcrafted leather jutti with dried canal silt marks.
- **Headwear**: Tightly tied ochre-rust Pagri turban.
- **Jewelry / Ornaments**: Heavy steel Sikh Karra on right wrist.
- **Scenes Worn**: `SC01`, `SC02`, `SC03`
- **Continuity Rationale**: Amar spends the day working the canal embankment and returns straight home without changing; the document stays secure in the same waistcoat pocket throughout.

### [COST_KABIR_01] Provincial City Denim & Leather Jacket
- **Character**: Kabir Singh (`CHAR_KABIR`)
- **Variant Number**: 1
- **Garments**: Slim-cut dark indigo denim jeans, beige knitted roll-neck, brown faux-leather biker jacket.
- **Regional Fabrics**: Processed synthetic denim and faux-leather, visually jarring against village handlooms.
- **Color Palette**: `#1e3a8a` (Deep Indigo), `#78350f` (Biker Brown), `#f5f5f4` (Beige).
- **Footwear**: Polished synthetic Chelsea boots.
- **Headwear**: None (modern styled gelled hair).
- **Jewelry / Ornaments**: Stainless steel digital watch, woven leather wrist cord.
- **Scenes Worn**: `SC02`, `SC03`
- **Continuity Rationale**: Kabir arrives directly from the regional bus terminal and carries his luggage into the field.
