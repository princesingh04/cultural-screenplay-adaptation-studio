### Scene 1: EXT. HOMESTEAD COURTYARD - EARLY MORNING
- **Location ID**: `LOC_COURTYARD`
- **Dramatic Purpose**: Amar receives the eviction notice and conceals it from Bebe to protect family peace.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere**: Dawn blue hour winter mist (kohrra) cutting across brick floors, cold breath condensation.
- **Camera Framing**: Wide establishing cinematic shot, low angle looking past the manji cot toward the deori gateway.
- **Character Blocking**: Amar stands center midground with back partly turned, clutching the sealed red-stamp letter; Bebe framed in the dark wooden deori doorway holding a warm brass cup; postman silhouetted by the iron gate.
- **Visual Reproduction Seed**: `749201`
- **Generative Prompt Specification**:

### Scene 2: INT. FAMILY HEARTH & KITCHEN - AFTERNOON
- **Location ID**: `LOC_KITCHEN_HEARTH`
- **Dramatic Purpose**: Kabir demands liquidation of ancestral acreage for city visa; Bebe defends the soil; Amar stays silent.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere**: Warm dusty afternoon raking sunlight through woodsmoke, high-contrast rim lighting.
- **Camera Framing**: Medium-close two-shot over Bebe's shoulder looking between the two brothers.
- **Character Blocking**: Kabir towering indignantly near the window light; Amar sitting exhausted near the woodpile clutching his coat; Bebe anchored beside the hearth flame.
- **Visual Reproduction Seed**: `749202`
- **Generative Prompt Specification**:

### Scene 3: EXT. THE DRY CANAL & ANCESTRAL GROVE - SUNSET
- **Location ID**: `LOC_DRY_CANAL`
- **Dramatic Purpose**: Amar reveals the ₹34 Lakh medical debt notice; Kabir renounces his departure to stand with his brother.
- **Lighting & Atmosphere**: Late sunset twilight with blazing orange-indigo horizon and rim light catching canal mist.
- **Camera Framing**: Cinematic golden-hour medium wide, two brothers standing on the canal bank against the dusk sky.
- **Character Blocking**: Kabir collapsed forward, head resting on Amar's shoulder; Amar holding him with weathered arms; the red-stamped paper clutched between them; barren branches of the banyan tree framing the shot.
- **Visual Reproduction Seed**: `749203`
